# How the data is collected

Each entry describes an **engine family**, not a single version: the Volkswagen EA888, for example, covers four generations and outputs from 160 to more than 330 CV. The most significant versions are listed in the dedicated table.

## Source verification status

Entries without a section of specific sources are marked as **awaiting documentary verification**. This does not mean they are wrong, but that individual figures are not yet linked to checkable documents. A generic link or a photograph is not proof of the technical specifications. Sources are added only after an actual comparison with the stated versions and years.

## Sources

Figures are compiled from manufacturers' public documentation, press kits and technical literature. Power and torque are given in CV (PS) and newton metres as declared for the market indicated; they may differ between Japan, Europe and the United States.

## What the fields mean

- **Displacement, bore and stroke** refer to the main version described in the entry.
- **Power** is the range between the least and the most powerful version of the family.
- **Rev limiter** is the typical stock value.
- **Limits and tuning** collects the thresholds commonly reported by tuners and enthusiasts. They are rough indications, not guarantees.

## Reliability index {#affidabilita}

The thermometer on each entry shows an **index from 0 to 100**. It is not a failure statistic, which for most engines is not public: it is an editorial rating based on documented faults, recalls, technical bulletins and the severity of their consequences.

| Index | Band | Criterion |
|---|---|---|
| 90–100 | Excellent | No recurring structural faults; very high documented mileages |
| 75–89 | Good | Only minor or accessory faults, solved with routine maintenance |
| 60–74 | Fair | At least one known costly fault, preventable with preventive work |
| 40–59 | Problematic | Recurring faults that can damage the engine even with regular maintenance |
| 0–39 | Critical | Widespread design faults with frequent serious failures |

The index rates the reliability of the stock engine, not its maintenance costs. Where long-term data is scarce (recent engines or very small production runs), the rating is marked as provisional.

## Translations

Italian is the reference text. English, French and Czech versions are written and reviewed separately; until an entry is translated, its page shows the Italian text with a notice.
