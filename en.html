Understanding the data

Engine-family specifications may span several versions and markets. Reliability scores are editorial estimates, not measured failure rates. Where individual references are missing, the article is explicitly marked as awaiting documentary verification. For service intervals and safety-critical decisions, always consult the official documentation for the exact engine code and model year.
